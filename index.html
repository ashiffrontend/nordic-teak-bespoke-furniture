Material Swatches & Timber Guide

We source solely responsibly harvested, kiln-dried hardwoods with extraordinary density, longevity, and tactile warmth. Select a timber to explore its characteristics.

Natural Golden Teak

Tectona Grandis • Warm Honey Gold
Natural Water & Weather Resistance 10 / 10
Janka Hardness (1,155 lbf) 9 / 10
Care: High natural oils preserve grain. Re-oil with Swedish botanical oil once annually for golden luster, or allow to weather into silver patina.

American Black Walnut

Juglans Nigra • Deep Chocolate & Mocha
Dimensional Stability 9.5 / 10
Grain Drama & Figure 10 / 10
Care: Velvety touch requiring organic beeswax seal. Shields against direct sun fading with a deep lustrous sheen that enriches over generations.

Nordic White Oak

Quercus Alba • Pale Biscuit & Straw
Impact & Wear Resistance 9.2 / 10
Minimalist Aesthetic Fit 10 / 10
Care: Finished with Scandinavian white-pigmented soap or matte oil. Prominent medullary ray flecks celebrate authentic raw timber simplicity.

East Indian Rosewood

Dalbergia Latifolia • Burgundy Purple Streaks
Density & Acoustic Rigidity 10 / 10
Heirloom Rarity 9.8 / 10
Care: Heavy, fragrant timber with subtle sweet resinous notes. Needs only light buffing with dry microfiber and biennial pure paste wax.

Sustainable Mahogany

Swietenia Macrophylla • Reddish-Brown Luster
Resistance to Swelling/Shrinkage 9.7 / 10
Joinery Stability 9.4 / 10
Care: Tight, interlocking grain that polishes to an exquisite optical depth. Finished in organic tung oil to protect against domestic spillages.

Scandinavian Pale Ash

Fraxinus Excelsior • Light Cream & Silver
Flexibility & Steam-Bending 10 / 10
Shock Absorbency 9.3 / 10
Care: Clean Nordic lines with pronounced grain. Ideal for sculptural bentwood chairs and dining room seating.
Heirloom Principles

Why Nordic Teak Bespoke

Six foundational tenets that distinguish our workshop from mass production furniture manufacturers.

100% Solid Hardwood

Zero veneer overlays, zero particleboard, and zero MDF. Every leg, rail, top, and drawer front is carved from solid, living timber slabs.

Handcrafted Joinery

Mortise and tenon, hand-chiseled dovetails, and wedged timber keys engineered to breathe with seasonal temperature and humidity changes.

FSC-Certified Eco Ethics

Every plank is tracked from certified managed plantations or reclaimed architectural beams. Three saplings planted for every tree harvested.

Made-to-Order Custom Spec

Tailor table lengths down to the millimetre, specify chamfer angles, cable recesses, or customize wood finishes to your interior scheme.

Zero VOC Organic Finishes

Preserved with food-safe botanical plant oils, natural tree resins, and local beeswax. Gentle on touch and completely toxin-free.

Lifetime Support & Care

Includes our Heirloom Registry certificate, initial complimentary care kit, and lifetime consultation with our master wood restoration team.

Atelier Heritage

The Art of Mindful Scandinavian Timber

Founded with an unbending devotion to honest materials, Nordic Teak Bespoke balances the Danish concept of hygge and Swedish lagom with uncompromising timber craftsmanship.

We harvest solely mature teak trees seasoned in ambient drying kilns for over 180 days to attain sub-8% moisture equilibrium. Every curve is planed by hand; every joint is shaped to endure for generations without mechanical screws or artificial polymers.
